Our Services

End-to-end support for your UK application

From your first question to your first week on campus — here is exactly how UK Study Abroad supports you at every stage.

Free Counselling & Course Selection

A relaxed, no-obligation conversation to understand your grades, budget, career goals and preferred UK cities before we suggest anything.

  • 30-45 minute consultation via WhatsApp call or video
  • Honest shortlist of 3-5 realistic universities
  • Clear next-step plan with no pressure to commit

University Application Support

We help you build a strong application — not just a submitted one — so you present your best self to admissions teams.

  • UCAS or direct university application guidance
  • Personal statement review and feedback
  • Document checklist: transcripts, certificates, references

Student Visa (Student Route) Guidance

Once your CAS is issued, we walk you through the Student Route visa application so nothing is left to guesswork.

  • CAS review and financial requirement explanation
  • Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) and fee breakdown
  • Biometric appointment and document checklist
See the full visa guide

IELTS & English Test Guidance

We help you understand exactly which English test and score your course and visa require — and how to prepare realistically.

  • UKVI IELTS vs Academic IELTS explained
  • CEFR B1/B2 requirements by course level
  • Recommended preparation resources and timelines
See IELTS details

Scholarship & Funding Guidance

We help you identify and apply for university scholarships, merit bursaries and relevant funding routes.

  • University-specific scholarship shortlists
  • Guidance on SACM-related funding questions
  • Application timeline planning around funding deadlines

Accommodation & Pre-Departure Support

Once your visa is approved, we help you prepare for departure and your first weeks on campus.

  • Accommodation shortlist near your university
  • Pre-departure checklist: banking, SIM, essentials
  • Airport arrival and first-week orientation tips
